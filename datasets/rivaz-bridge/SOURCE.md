# Rivaz bridge and village training survey

Source: Pix4D example projects, Rivaz, Switzerland.
https://support.pix4d.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000235126

Download: https://data.pix4d.com/misc/example_datasets/Pix4Dsurvey_example_Rivaz.zip

Courtesy of Pix4D / https://pix4d.com

The provider permits personal or professional training. For commercial or promotional use, its source page requires the linked attribution above and directs users to contact its marketing team for details.

The source contains 101 geotagged aerial photographs captured by an eBee X with a senseFly S.O.D.A. camera, along with separate terrestrial scan products. This local run processes the aerial photographs only. Copies are reduced to a maximum dimension of 2400 pixels with EXIF retained. Outputs are a demonstration reconstruction and do not include the provider's terrestrial laser scans.
